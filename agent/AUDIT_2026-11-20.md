# Audit of the evaluation setup (2026-11-20)

Status: proposed. Each item is a measurement, not a reading of the code. Evidence and script:
`agent/evidence/audit-2026-11-20/` (`audit.py`); evaluation fleet, sigma 0.25, history rows 576, seeds 0 to 19 of each population
(A holdout, B altB, C popC, D popD), run with `risk_aware_rule_based` and `fixed_20`.

## 1. latency_violation

- **Threshold.** `latency_violation` is true when `latency_now > LATENCY_LIMIT_FACTOR * latency_target` = 1.5 x 15 ms = **22.5 ms**.
- **Utilisation equivalent.** With `latency = 2.0 / (1 - rho)`: `rho = 1 - 2.0 / 22.5` = **0.9111** (`violation_rho_threshold`).
  The environment caps `rho` at `rho_max` = 0.98.
- **Combined MB/s.** 0.9111 x `BW_TOTAL_MBPS` (200) = **182.2 MB/s** of foreground plus delivered rebuild rate; the cap is 196 MB/s.
- **Intervals within 5 MB/s of 182.2** (combined foreground plus delivered, over the intervals of the 20 episodes):

| Population | rule: within 5 MB/s | rule: above | `fixed_20`: within 5 MB/s | `fixed_20`: above |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 1.54% | 3.58% | 0.11% | 1.05% |
| B | 1.92% | 2.79% | 0.28% | 0.65% |
| C | 2.37% | 2.92% | 0.49% | 0.88% |
| D | 2.04% | 3.92% | 0.52% | 1.15% |

  No row of any population's foreground trace alone is within 5 MB/s of 182.2; every interval near the
  threshold is near it because of the rebuild rate the safety filter or the policy adds.

## 2. degraded_cost

On every interval of every audited episode the reward minus its latency terms equals -`beta` (0.1) exactly: `degraded_cost` is 1 on
all 10405, 34761, 9523, 34746, 10570, 34763, 11299 and 34744 intervals (rule and `fixed_20`, A to D). So `beta * steps` is the
completion-time term of the reward, and the reward is `-(alpha * D + gamma * V) - 0.1 * steps`.

## 3. hazard_uncertainty

The default `k` of `RiskAwareRuleBased` is 0, so the rule, the hand-built seasonal rule, the predictive controller and the
look-ahead oracle do not read `hazard_uncertainty` in their decisions. It is read by: the diagnostic `risk_aware_rule_based k=1`
(`diagnostic_policies`), and, as an input feature (field 6 of the observation), by PPO and forecast-PPO and by the residual
Q-policy. The inflated hazard enters the rule's risk floor only with `k` other than 0.

## 4. The hazard observation clip (1.8e-4 per hour)

- **Scenarios.** In **2 of the 20 scenarios of every population** (seeds 9 and 17; the fleet depends on the scenario seed, not on the
  workload) the reported hazard at reset is above 1.8e-4 per hour, and it is above it at some step of the episode in the same 2 scenarios (2 of 20 for both
  policies on A, B, C and D).
- **What a policy cannot see.** In those scenarios the observation shows 1.8e-4. At that value the largest action is still feasible
  against the risk budget at full remaining work, so a policy never sees an infeasible budget, and its risk floor computed from the
  clipped hazard differs from the safety filter's: in 3.3% to 3.6% of the rule's intervals (A 3.34%, B 3.63%, C 3.46%, D 3.32%) and
  1.1% to 1.2% of `fixed_20`'s intervals (the filter, which uses the reported hazard, is not affected).

## 5. Terminal failures

No `terminal_failure` and no `horizon_expired` in any episode of any committed evidence: 7620 episodes in 31 files, plus all 7280
episodes of the objective-sensitivity runs and all 160 audited episodes completed (`termination_reasons.csv`).
